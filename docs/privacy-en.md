# LineLog (记管家) · Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 20, 2026

LineLog (the "App") is developed and operated by an independent developer ("we"). The App is built privacy-first from the ground up: **we do not collect any of your data.**

## What We Collect

**Nothing.**

- All your entries, to-dos, reminders and photos are stored only on your device, and — if you enable it — in your own private iCloud storage
- We operate no servers; we have no way to access any of your content
- The App contains no analytics SDKs, no ad SDKs, and no third-party trackers

## Permissions

| Permission | Purpose | Required? |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Fire reminders you create yourself | No — declining only disables reminders |
| Photo Library (add) | Attach photos/videos to entries | No — declining only disables attachments |
| iCloud | Sync data across your own devices | No — the App is fully usable without it |

## Storage & Sync

- **Local-first**: all data lives on your device by default; deleting the App deletes it permanently
- **iCloud sync** (optional): data syncs across devices sharing your Apple ID via Apple CloudKit, stored in your private iCloud space under [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). We cannot access it
- **Export**: you can export everything at any time via Me → Data → Export All Data (JSON + original attachments)

## Data Deletion

- Individual items: swipe to delete within the App
- Everything: Me → Data → Erase All Data (with confirmation); deletes locally and across iCloud

## In-App Purchases

The lifetime unlock is processed entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment or account information.

## Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we collect no personal information from anyone, including children.
