# LineLog · Support

## FAQ

### How does the trial work?
The App is free to download with a **30-day full-featured trial**. When it ends, nothing is deleted — every entry stays viewable forever; you just can't add new ones. A one-time ¥25 purchase unlocks everything. No subscriptions, no ads.

### Where is my data stored? Will I lose it on a new phone?
Data lives on-device by default; with iCloud signed in, it syncs automatically across all devices sharing your Apple ID. Sign in on a new phone and it syncs itself.

### Do attachments (photos/videos) sync via iCloud?
In version 1.0 attachments stay on-device and do not sync via iCloud. You can export original files anytime via Me → Data → Export All Data. Cross-device attachment sync is planned.

### Where do completed to-dos go?
Checking off a to-do automatically creates an entry on that day's timeline and Today page; unchecking removes it again — no duplicates pile up.

### Strong vs. regular reminders?
Strong reminders (the orange toggle in the reminder list) use time-sensitive notifications: they break through Focus Mode and re-ring every 15 minutes (up to 3 times) until handled.

### How do lunar reminders repeat?
They repeat yearly by lunar date, automatically converted to that year's solar calendar; if a year lacks the matching leap month, a normal month is used.

### What is a Time Capsule?
Seal a message for a future date (e.g. 6 months). While sealed it's invisible everywhere; on the reveal day an "unseal" card appears on Today.

### How do I erase all data?
Me → Data → Erase All Data (double confirmation). This deletes data locally and across all your iCloud devices — export a backup first.
